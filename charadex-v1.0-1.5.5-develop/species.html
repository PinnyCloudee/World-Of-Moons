About Moontails

"Long ago, on a desolate little planet far, far away, lived a lonely godess. Luvo, once a young meteor had grown to become a full sized moon, orbiting an empty planet. However, as her light hit its cold surface, life blossomed once more. The reeds grew tall, gently swaying with the river song; trees reached for the sky, cradling the stars in their branches. When Luvo first saw what she had created, she wept with joy, bringing the first meteor shower. Glowing fragments of rock crashes into the land. And one by one, they cracked open; hatching like eggs all over the planet. Some landed on mountains, Feathered wings unfurled as bright cheeps reached the skies. Some landed in the deep forests, horns like tree roots nestled under the cover of leaves. And some landed in the plains, dancing happily under the moonlight. And thus, the first moontails were born. The titans, majestic is both size and aura. But soon, the godess had spent all of her energy. Going into a deep slumber, dreaming of the creatures she created and their adventures. Its been thousands of years, and the titants are long gone; only remembered by their direct decendants, the royals. And yet the moontails persist. These days, the once mountain bound Airborn found joy and love in the forests and plains. The shy Elves now live across the land, no longer hiding in bushes and shadows. And those from the plains? Well, they explored, mapped out the world."
